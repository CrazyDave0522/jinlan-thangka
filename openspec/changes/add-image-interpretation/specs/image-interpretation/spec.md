## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Evidence-based image interpretation

The system SHALL support image-based artwork identification and subject, deity, and style explanations while requiring evidence for a definite artwork identity and acknowledging uncertain or unmatched results.

#### Scenario: Image lacks a confirmed archive match

- **WHEN** a submitted image lacks sufficient evidence to identify a specific archived artwork
- **THEN** the answer does not assert a definite artwork identity and distinguishes tentative interpretation from supported archive facts.
